# ⚙️ Account Settings & Security

## 🎯 1. Business Objective
The Account Settings dashboard equips users with critical security controls to safeguard their account credentials and active sessions.

---

## 🔒 2. Password Modification Flow

### Business Rules:
1. **Current Password Validation**: Users must provide their active password prior to submitting a replacement.
2. **Password Complexity**: Minimum 8 characters, containing at least 1 uppercase letter, 1 lowercase letter, 1 number, and 1 special character.
3. **Global Sign-Out**: Successfully updating a password automatically triggers [Session Revocation in Auth](/docs/auth/oauth-sso/session-management) for all other devices.

---

## 🛡️ 3. MFA Management & Active Device Audit

- **MFA Toggle**: Clicking "Enable MFA" redirects directly to the setup workflow in [Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA)](/docs/auth/login-flow/mfa).
- **Active Devices List**: Displays IP address, User-Agent, location estimate, and last active time. Users can click "Revoke Session" to terminate any specific device.
